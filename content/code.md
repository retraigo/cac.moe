# Code of CAC

1. We are committed to providing a friendly, safe and welcoming environment for all, regardless of any individual characteristic.
2. Please be kind and courteous. There’s no need to be mean or rude.
3. Insulting, demeaning, or harassing someone is not welcome behavior. This includes:
   - Violence or threats of violence
   - Sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise discriminatory language
   - Sharing sexually explicit or violent material
   - Doxing
   - Personal insults
   - Inappropriate photography or recording
   - Inappropriate physical interaction
   - Disruption of community events
4. Respect differences in opinion.
