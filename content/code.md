# Code of CAC

**1. We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all, regardless of any individual characteristic.**
* Please converse primarily in English so everyone feels included and can participate freely.
* Respect differences in opinion - healthy discussion is encouraged, but there is no need to be mean or rude.

**2. Insulting, demeaning, or harassing someone is strictly prohibited and will result in an immediate ban. As this is a family-friendly and kid-friendly club, we maintain strict boundaries. This includes:**
* Violence or threats of violence
* Sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise derogatory and discriminatory language
* Sharing sexually explicit, NSFW, nudity, or violent/gore material
* Doxing or sharing anyone's private personal information without consent
* Personal insults, bullying, and toxic behaviour
* Inappropriate photography, recording, or unwelcome physical interaction at in-person meetups
* Disruption of community events or club activities

**3. Respect community spaces, sub-groups, and channel purposes.**
* Keep conversations relevant to each sub-group's stated topic to foster productive discussions.
* No spamming: consolidate your thoughts into a single message when possible.
* Contact an admin prior to posting self-promotions, surveys, or major announcements.

**4. Follow anime and content-sharing etiquette.**
* Use the spoiler hiding tool when discussing plot details on WhatsApp, or move the discussion to dedicated Discord spoiler channels.
* Do not promote or share piracy in any CAC space.
* Always credit the original creator when reposting fan art or content.

**5. Do not share or promote AI generated content.**
* Do not use generative AI (Gen AI) to create, post, or share artwork or content. We value human creativity and authentic expression.

---
*Please contact any admin if you have any questions or concerns.*  
*We hope you have a great time with us!*
